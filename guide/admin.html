Guide

Gym admin

Staff, roles, map, settings, moderation.

Invite staff

  1. Users → Invite member.
  2. Enter the email and pick a role; they get an invitation link.

Roles and permissions

  1. Users → Roles & permissions: create roles and tick what each may do.

Draw the gym map

  1. Map editor: pick a location, create the floor plan.
  2. Draw walls, mats and the entrance; Save.

Branding

  1. Settings → Branding: app logo, browser icon, logo on route signs, cover image.

Grading scales

  1. Settings → Grading scales: the scale for routes and for boulders.

TV screen

  1. Settings → TV screen: what the gym TV shows.

Gym TV

  1. Open /<gym>/tv on a screen in the gym.

Legal notice and privacy

  1. Settings → Legal notice and Privacy page fill the gym pages below, or link your own.

Legal notice page

  1. Public at /<gym>/imprint.

Handle content reports

  1. Moderation: reported reviews and content.
  2. Hide or keep it; the reporter is notified (DSA).

Reviews

  1. Reviews: read, filter and hide route reviews.

Competitions

Enter a competition or run one: categories, judging, live ranking.

API

Every endpoint of the Gripello API, as served by gripello.app.

# Setting routes

> For setters: routes, signs, walls, inventory, defects.

## Staff tools

1. Tap **Staff tools** in the menu. What you see depends on your role.

<shot name="manage" title="Staff tools">



</shot>

## Add a route

1. **Manage routes → Create route**.
2. Set name, type, grade, anchor, location, setter, date and colour, then **Create**.
3. Put it on its wall under **Routes & resets** (below).

<shot name="route-create" title="Add a route">



</shot>

## Edit or archive routes

1. Tap the pencil to edit a route.
2. Select routes and **Archive** them when they come off the wall.
3. **Import routes** reads a spreadsheet.

<shot name="manage-routes" title="Edit or archive routes">



</shot>

## Print QR tags

1. **Manage routes**: select the routes, tap **Print**.
2. Pick language, layout and what to show, then **Export** a PDF; every tag carries the QR code climbers scan.

<shot name="print" title="Print QR tags">



</shot>

## Place routes on walls

1. **Routes & resets**: pick the hall.
2. Drag routes from the list onto a wall; **Save**.

<shot name="manage-map" title="Place routes on walls">



</shot>

## Take inventory

1. **Inventory**: choose a location and tap **Start scanning**.
2. Scan every route sign.
3. **Finish inventory** archives the routes that were not found.

<shot name="manage-inventory" title="Take inventory">



</shot>

## Fix defects

1. Problems climbers report land in **Tasks**.
2. Drag a task to In progress, Waiting or Done; the reporter is told when it is fixed.

<shot name="manage-tasks" title="Fix defects">



</shot>

## Plan work

1. **Tasks → New task**.
2. Pick Reset, Maintenance or Other, give it a title and priority, the location or wall, who does it and by when.

<shot name="task-new" title="Plan work">



</shot>

## Analytics

1. Routes set, reviews, stars, grade distribution and how long routes stay up.

</shot>

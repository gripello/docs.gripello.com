# Competitions

> Enter a competition or run one: categories, judging, live ranking.

## Find a competition

1. Open **Competitions** for upcoming, running and past events.

<shot name="competitions" title="Find a competition">



</shot>

## Enter a competition

1. Open it, fill in **Take part** and tap **Sign up**.
2. Log your attempts on your phone while it runs; the ranking updates live.

<shot name="competition" title="Enter a competition">



</shot>

## Rules

1. **Rules** explains scoring, categories and fair play for this event.

<shot name="competition-rules" title="Rules">



</shot>

## Create a competition

1. **Staff tools → Competitions → New competition**.
2. Pick discipline and scoring format, set the time, then add categories and boulders or routes.

<shot name="competition-new" title="Create a competition">



</shot>

## Run it

1. Move it through Draft → Registration open → Running → Ended → Results published.
2. **Participants**: check climbers in. **Export** the results.

<shot name="manage-competition" title="Run it">



</shot>

## Judge

1. **Judging**: pick a boulder, then count tries, zone and top per climber.

<shot name="manage-competition-judge" title="Judge">



</shot>

## Live ranking on a screen

1. Open **TV view** on a screen at the venue; it refreshes by itself.

</shot>

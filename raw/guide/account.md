# Account

> Sign up, sign in, password, two-factor, your data.

## Create an account

1. Open **Sign in** and tap **Create account**.
2. Enter first and last name, username, email and password.
3. Open the link in the email to verify your address.
4. Sign-up only shows when the operator allows it; otherwise ask your gym for an invitation.

<shot name="register" title="Create an account">



</shot>

## Sign in

1. Sign in with username or email and password, or with a passkey.
2. With two-factor on, enter the code from your authenticator app next.

<shot name="login" title="Sign in">



</shot>

## Reset your password

1. On the sign-in page tap **Reset password**.
2. Enter the email of your account and tap **Submit**.
3. Open the link in the email (valid 30 minutes) and choose a new password. All other sessions are signed out.

<shot name="reset" title="Reset your password">



</shot>

## Your account

1. Tap your name at the bottom of the menu.
2. Profile, friends, staff tools and the gyms you belong to.

<shot name="account" title="Your account">



</shot>

## Edit your profile and email

1. Open **Account → Settings → Profile**.
2. Change name, avatar or banner.
3. A new email address needs your password and is active once you open the confirmation link sent to it.

<shot name="settings-profile" title="Edit your profile and email">



</shot>

## Language and theme

1. **Settings → Preferences**: language, light/dark theme.

<shot name="settings-preferences" title="Language and theme">



</shot>

## Change your password

1. Open **Settings → Security**.
2. Enter the current and the new password, then save.

<shot name="settings-security" title="Change your password">



</shot>

## Turn on two-factor sign-in

1. **Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication**.
2. Set up an authenticator app or add a passkey.
3. Store the recovery codes somewhere safe.

## Sign out other devices

1. **Settings → Security** lists active sessions; sign out the ones you do not recognise.

## Download your data

1. **Settings → Security → Download your data → Email me a link**.
2. The link in the email works for 7 days.

## Delete your account

1. **Settings → Security → Delete account**.
2. Routes and reviews you created stay; your profile, logbook and memberships are removed.

## Notifications

1. **Settings → Notifications**: turn on push for this device and pick the topics.

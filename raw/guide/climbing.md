# Climbing

> Find routes, log ascents, follow friends.

## Find your gym

1. Open Gripello and search for your gym.
2. Open it; Gripello remembers it for next time.

<shot name="home" title="Find your gym">



</shot>

## See what is new

1. The gym overview shows new routes per wall, your progress and running competitions.

<shot name="overview" title="See what is new">



</shot>

## Find a route

1. Open **Routes**.
2. Filter by type, grade, colour, location or wall, or search by name, setter or grade.

<shot name="routes" title="Find a route">



</shot>

## Find routes on the map

1. Open **Map** and pick a hall.
2. Tap a wall to list its routes.

<shot name="map" title="Find routes on the map">



</shot>

## Scan a route sign

1. Tap the scan button or open **Scan**.
2. Point the camera at the QR code on the route sign; the route opens.

<shot name="scan" title="Scan a route sign">



</shot>

## Log an ascent

1. Open the route and tap **Log ascent**.
2. Pick Flash, Top or Attempt, the number of attempts and the date; add a private note if you like.
3. Save; the ascent lands in your logbook.

<shot name="tick" title="Log an ascent">



</shot>

## Rate and review a route

1. Open the route and tap **Write a review**.
2. Give stars, pick the grade it felt like, add a comment, **Submit**.

<shot name="review" title="Rate and review a route">



</shot>

## Report a problem with a route

1. Open the route, tap **⋯** and **Report a problem**.
2. Pick what is wrong (loose hold, missing tag …) and add details or a photo.
3. The setters get it as a task.

<shot name="defect" title="Report a problem with a route">



</shot>

## Keep a logbook

1. Open **Logbook**: sessions, timeline, stats, projects and badges.
2. Switch between boulders and routes and the time range at the top.

<shot name="logbook" title="Keep a logbook">



</shot>

## Follow friends

1. Open **Friends** and search for a climber.
2. Tap **Follow**; climbers who approve followers see a request first.

<shot name="friends" title="Follow friends">



</shot>

## Follow the feed

1. Open **Feed**: sends of the people you follow and new routes on walls you follow.

<shot name="feed" title="Follow the feed">



</shot>

## Climber profiles

1. Tap a climber anywhere to see their sends, stats and achievements.

<shot name="climber" title="Climber profiles">



</shot>

## Leaderboard

1. Open **Leaderboard** for the gym ranking; switch boulders/routes and the period.
2. Hide yourself in **Settings → Privacy**.

<shot name="leaderboard" title="Leaderboard">



</shot>

## Opening hours and contact

1. Open **Info** for opening hours, contact, facilities and address.

<shot name="info" title="Opening hours and contact">



</shot>
